Privacy policy

This website is based in Germany, so its privacy policy follows German law. If the language versions differ, the German version applies.

In short: this website uses no cookies, no analytics and no ads, and loads nothing from Google. I only process your data if you write to me or book a call.

1. Controller

Marta Alarcón Rodríguez
Etzelstraße 231
50739 Köln
Germany
Email: martaalarcon22@icloud.com

2. Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA); a copy is also available on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit it, the server automatically logs technical data such as your IP address, date and time, the page visited, your browser and the referring site. This is needed to deliver the website and protect it against attacks (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Both providers are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and keep these logs only for a limited time.

3. Fonts

All fonts are hosted on this website itself. No connection is made to Google or any other font server.

4. Language preference

If you choose a language, your browser stores it (localStorage, key “bymarta-lang”) to remember it next time. It contains no personal data and is never sent to a server. You can delete it in your browser settings.

5. Contact form

If you write to me using the form, your name, email address, topic and message are sent to my inbox via Web3Forms (Web3Creative, India). Web3Forms uses providers in the EU, the USA and other countries under EU Standard Contractual Clauses and keeps submissions for up to three years. I use your data only to reply, based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and, for a project or a role, for steps prior to a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). I delete it once it is no longer needed, unless the law requires me to keep it.

6. Email

If you email me, your data reaches my iCloud (Apple) inbox and I use it only to reply (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

7. Call booking, WhatsApp and other links

The buttons for the virtual coffee (Cal.com, Inc., USA), WhatsApp (WhatsApp Ireland Ltd.), LinkedIn and GitHub are plain links: nothing loads until you click. When you open them you move to the provider's site, where its own privacy policy applies. If you book a call, I receive your name, email address and chosen time, and the appointment is saved to my iCloud calendar.

8. Your rights

You can ask me at any time for access to your data, or to correct, delete, restrict, transfer it or object to its processing (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and withdraw your consent with effect for the future. Just write to me. You can also complain to a data protection authority; mine is the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (Düsseldorf).
