DEMYA Plan de Marketing Digital

A full digital marketing plan for DEMYA, a group of aesthetic medicine and plastic surgery clinics: website and SEO audit, social media, competitors, SWOT, SMART goals, action plan and KPIs. Updated with October 2026 data and turned into an interactive web report, with a PDF version.

View the web report PDF (Spanish)

plan-marketing-demya

1 / 5

01 My role
Analysis, strategy and presentation of the plan, from the audit to the KPIs.
02 The challenge
Turning a lot of information (website, SEO, social media and competitors) into a plan a clinic could understand and put into practice.
03 What I did
An audit with Screaming Frog, PageSpeed Insights and Ahrefs, a SWOT, SMART goals, an action plan and KPIs, updated with October 2026 data.
04 The result
A 16-chapter interactive web report with a print-ready PDF, where every finding comes with its screenshot and recommendation.

Highlights

  • 16 chapters, from situation analysis to KPIs, with a fixed table of contents
  • Audit SEO and performance with Screaming Frog, PageSpeed Insights and Ahrefs
  • Evidence annotated screenshots with numbered frames and enlarged details
  • Web + PDF one source, responsive on screen and print-ready in A4

Built with HTML5 · CSS3 · JavaScript · Screaming Frog · PageSpeed Insights · Ahrefs
