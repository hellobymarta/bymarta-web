Golden Chance Senior

In development

A landing page for a new senior living community in northern Spain, backed by Golden Chance. It presents a way of living built on independence, community, security and the future, in Spanish and English, with an enquiry form for anyone who wants to learn more.

The site is live and keeps evolving: the mobile experience and new content are being added step by step.

golden-chance-senior.vercel.app

1 / 6

01 My role
Brand, copy, art direction and development of the landing page, working directly with the client.
02 The challenge
Presenting a residential project that doesn't exist yet, building trust with older people and their families, in two languages.
03 What I did
A bilingual (ES/EN) landing page with clear messages on independence, community and security, an enquiry form with privacy consent, and deployment on Vercel with self-hosted fonts.
04 The result
The site is already live as the first point of contact for interested people. I'm now adding the mobile experience and new content.

Highlights

  • Brand design, copy and art direction, from start to finish
  • ES / EN bilingual, with links that open straight in English
  • Enquiries a form that goes straight to the client's inbox, with privacy consent
  • Vercel deployment with caching and security headers, and self-hosted fonts

Built with HTML5 · CSS3 · JavaScript · FormSubmit · Vercel
